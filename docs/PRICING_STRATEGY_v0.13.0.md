# Sonkupik Live Pricing Strategy — v0.13.0

## Implemented launch offer

- Full-feature trial: 30 days
- Full Version: Rp249.000
- Billing: one-time perpetual licence
- Entitlement: one computer
- Payment: Midtrans QRIS through the MasArray licence server
- No subscription or automatic renewal

## Product positioning

Rp249.000 is an appropriate launch price while Sonkupik Live is establishing its
reputation for long-session stability, easy routing and support. It is low enough
for Indonesian streamers and home-studio users who currently open a full DAW only
for live enhancement, while still communicating that this is professional audio
software rather than a free utility.

## Recommended price path

1. **Launch / early commercial release: Rp249.000**
   - Keep this price through the first stable public cohort.
   - Use the 30-day trial to demonstrate the actual sound and workflow.

2. **Regular price after production proof: Rp349.000–Rp399.000**
   - Appropriate after the installer, VB-CABLE-assisted setup, recovery flow,
     long-session anti-crackle testing and customer support are proven.

3. **Future Pro edition: Rp499.000–Rp699.000**
   - Appropriate only after real external VST3 hosting, plug-in scan/recovery,
     crash isolation and reusable rack presets are production-ready.

The desktop client hard-rejects any checkout whose server amount differs from the
compiled Rp249.000 launch price. A future price change therefore requires an
intentional app release and matching server catalogue update.
